    What this is. A teaching simulation. Lumora Bank, Teleqo Mobile, the crews, the customers, the cards and the amounts are all fictional, and every card and phone number is masked. Nothing here touches a real system. “Controls on” models runtime identity controls for AI agents (verified agent identity, a mandate delegated by an accountable human, per-action policy, behavior analytics, quarantine and a kill switch), the class of control delivered by IBM Verify Identity Protection with Agentic Runtime Security, plus a SIM-swap risk signal that the mobile operator shares with the bank. It is an independent illustration, not IBM software.
